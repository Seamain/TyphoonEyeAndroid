TyphoonEye is a lightweight, open-source real-time typhoon tracking and weather alert application for Android.

Features:
• Live Typhoon Tracking: Monitor active tropical cyclones with interactive map rendering.
• Weather Alerts & Distance Calculations: Automatic distance calculation relative to user location.
• Customizable Map Basemaps: Built-in support for vector and raster map styles powered by MapLibre.
• 100% Free & Open Source: Completely free of non-free dependencies, analytics, or proprietary trackers.
